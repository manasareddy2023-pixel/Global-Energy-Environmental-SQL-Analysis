# Global-Energy-Environmental-SQL-Analysis
A MySQL-based data analysis project exploring global energy consumption, production, GDP, population, and emissions using SQL queries and data analysis techniques.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes global energy and environmental data across different countries and years.

The analysis focuses on understanding:

- Energy consumption
- Energy production
- CO₂ and other emissions
- GDP
- Population
- Per-capita energy and emission measures
- Relationships between economic growth, energy usage, population, and emissions

The project uses multiple related datasets and SQL queries to generate meaningful country-wise and year-wise insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze global energy consumption and production using SQL.
- Compare energy production and consumption across countries and years.
- Identify trends in GDP, population, energy usage, and emissions.
- Analyze country-wise emission patterns.
- Compare energy consumption and production on a per-capita basis.
- Understand the relationship between energy usage and economic output.
- Generate meaningful insights from real-world energy data.

## 🔍 SQL Analysis

The project covers four major areas of analysis.

### 1. General & Comparative Analysis

The analysis includes:

- Countries with the highest total emissions.
- Top countries by GDP in the most recent year.
- Comparison of energy production and consumption by country and year.
- Contribution of different energy types to total emissions.

### 2. Trend Analysis Over Time

The project analyzes:

- Global emissions trends over the years.
- GDP changes across countries and years.
- Relationship between population growth and total emissions.
- Energy consumption trends for major economies.
- Changes in per-capita emissions over time.

### 3. Ratio & Per-Capita Analysis

The analysis calculates:

- Energy consumption per person.
- Energy production per person.
- Energy consumption relative to GDP.
- Changes in per-capita emissions.

### 4. Global Comparisons

The project compares:

- Average GDP by year.
- Average emissions by year.
- Average population by year.
- Energy consumption relative to GDP across countries.

## 📊 Key Insights

### Emissions

- Global emissions increased every year from 2020 to 2023.
- Total emissions increased from **67,852 in 2020 to 74,161 in 2023**.
- CO₂ emissions were the largest contributor among the listed emission categories.
- Coal and coke were the second-largest contributors.
- Natural gas had the lowest contribution among the listed energy types.

### GDP

The analysis of the most recent year showed:

1. China
2. United States
3. India
4. Japan
5. Germany

as the top five countries by GDP in the analyzed dataset.

### Energy Consumption

Energy consumption trends varied across major economies.

For example:

- China's energy consumption increased from **156 in 2020 to 177 in 2023**.
- Germany's consumption decreased from **13 in 2020 to 11 in 2023**.

### Population & Emissions

The analysis shows that countries with larger populations generally have higher total emissions.

### Per-Capita Analysis

Per-capita calculations help compare energy consumption and production between countries while considering differences in population size.
